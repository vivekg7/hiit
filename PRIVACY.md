# Privacy

HIIT Timer collects nothing.

- **No network.** The app does not declare the `INTERNET` permission, so Android
  will not let it open a network connection of any kind. There are no ads,
  analytics, crash reporters or accounts.
- **What is stored, and where.** Your saved workouts and the three cue settings
  (beeps, voice, vibration) are kept in the app's private storage on your phone.
  Two short beep sounds are generated into the app's cache. Nothing else is
  written.
- **Voice announcements** use the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone.
  The app hands it only fixed words ("Work", "Rest", "Halfway", "Last round") and
  the exercise names you typed. What that engine does is up to the engine; the
  default Google and open-source engines speak offline.
- **Backups.** If you use Android's own device backup, your workouts are included
  in it like any app's data. That is handled by Android and your backup provider,
  not by this app.
- **Uninstalling** removes everything the app stored.
